Applied Machine Learning Engineering Specification

Structured technical analysis conforming to production machine learning standards.

STEP 01

Problem Formulation

OPERATIONAL_CHALLENGE

Manual verification of public health compliance in crowded facilities is non-scalable, labor-intensive, and prone to human inspection fatigue.

STEP 02

Data Ingestion & Feature Space

DATASET_TELEMETRY

Annotated multi-class image dataset with precise bounding box coordinates categorized into three states: masked, unmasked, and incorrectly worn masks.

STEP 03

Preprocessing & Methodology

FEATURE_ENGINEERING

Engineered robust preprocessing pipeline leveraging mosaic augmentation, color jittering, spatial scaling, and normalized bounding box coordinate conversions.

STEP 04

Model Architecture & Training

ALGORITHM_DESIGN

Fine-tuned YOLOv5 architecture featuring CSPDarknet53 backbone and Path Aggregation Network (PANet) neck for multi-scale feature localization.

STEP 05

Validation & Evaluation Protocol

PERFORMANCE_METRICS

Validated against mean Average Precision (mAP@0.5 and mAP@0.5:0.95), inference latency (milliseconds per frame), and precision-recall trade-offs.

STEP 06

Empirical Results & Discoveries

FINDINGS

Demonstrated accurate multi-target localization in high-density frames with minimal latency, distinguishing subtle mask placement variations.

STEP 07

Production & Business Impact

STRATEGIC_VALUE

Enables contactless, automated compliance telemetry across transit hubs and commercial facilities without requiring on-site personnel.
